How to Install LunarDrift on Google TV

Follow this simple step-by-step walkthrough to install and set up LunarDrift on your Google TV or Android TV device.

1

Open Google Play Store on Your TV

Turn on your Google TV / Android TV device. From the main home screen, navigate over to the Apps tab and open the Google Play Store.

2

Install the Downloader App

Use the search function in Google Play to search for Downloader by AFTVnews. Select the app from the search results and click Install.

3

Enter the LunarDrift URL

Launch the Downloader app and grant requested storage permissions. In the URL bar, type: https://lunardrift.watch and click Go to start the download.

4

Complete Installation & Launch

When the package finishes downloading, click Install on the Android installer prompt. Once done, launch LunarDrift directly from your TV apps screen and start streaming!
